Tissue shape

Drag an outline point to reshape the tissue (it becomes custom) · double-click the outline to add a point · click a point + Del to remove it.

Drag to rotate · scroll to zoom · right-drag or Space+drag to pan.

Domain & resolution

Resizing scales the roots and trunk calibre with the object; then add generations, growth steps, attraction points or resolution for more detail. Finer grids solve more slowly (3-D cost grows with n³). Zoom the view to inspect any part.

Roots (initial condition)

Each organ starts with its anatomical artery and vein entry (renal hilum, aortic root, porta hepatis, circle of Willis…). Drag ▲ / ▼ to move a root and its ● handle to aim it.

Branching (fractal skeleton)

Asymmetry γ and the Murray exponent set the child calibres and angles in 3-D as in 2-D; each split turns its plane by the twist so the tree fills the volume from the first generations.

Space filling

Grow the skeleton's tips on until the tree fills the tissue.

Field & objective

Computed in 3-D: pressure, Darcy flux and time-of-flight on the voxel bed (colour range clipped to the 2–98 % percentiles).
